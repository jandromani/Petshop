# Company constitution

## Mission
Help independent people turn retirement income into freedom of place.

## Brand
Freedom, curiosity, agency and clarity. Never geriatric. Never patronizing. Never fear-led.

## Truth
No live claim without evidence. No LLM-authored prices. Provider data is stored with timestamp and provenance.

## Money
User value ranks before commission. Revenue can break ties between materially equivalent choices; it cannot manufacture a recommendation.

## Risk
Agents may propose and operate within bounded budgets. They may not sign contracts, alter legal commitments, publish unverified prices or silently promote their own outputs to company truth.

## Judging
Actors do not approve their own work. Important artifacts are evaluated by independent judges with only the artifact and evidence they need.
